Services

What we do

Asphalt shingle roofs, gutters, and the small parts that cause big leaks. Houses, garages, and sheds.

Roof replacement

Full tear-off down to the wood. New underlayment, ice and water shield along the edges, new vents and flashing, then new shingles. If any decking is bad, we show you before we replace it.

Roof repair

Missing shingles, lifted tabs, cracked pipe boots, worn flashing. A lot of leaks come down to one bad spot. We find it and fix it.

Storm and leak repair

Wind took some shingles or water is coming through the ceiling? Call us. We tarp it to stop the damage, then come back and fix it right.

Gutters

Seamless gutters, downspouts, and guards. Hung with the right pitch so water runs away from your foundation, not into it.

Roof inspections

Buying, selling, or just wondering how much life your roof has left? We go over it top to bottom and give you a written report with photos.

Vents, flashing, and skylights

Chimney flashing, leaky skylights, attic vents. Small parts that cause a lot of leaks and ice dams when they go bad.
