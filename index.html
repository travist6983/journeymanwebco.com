07 · Straight answers

How long does it take?

Most sites launch within 1 to 2 weeks of receiving your content. The biggest variable is how fast I get your photos and information.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The site is yours once the build is paid for, on either plan. On a monthly plan that's after months, or sooner if you pay off the rest of the build.

Do you do SEO or ads?

Every site gets search-friendly foundations and a Google Business Profile cleanup. I don't sell ranking guarantees. Be wary of anyone who does.

What if I have no photos or copy?

I can write your copy and coordinate a local photographer as add-ons. You do the work. I'll make it look like it.

Why do I need Hosting & Care?

A website needs somewhere to live, security updates, and backups. Hosting & Care covers all of that plus small edits each month, so you never have to log in to anything. After the first months it's month to month, and if you leave I hand over every file.
