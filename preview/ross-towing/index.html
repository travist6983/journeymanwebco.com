Environmental spill response · MDOT certified

One of only two licensed towing companies permitted to respond anywhere in Michigan

Ross Towing is a licensed and permitted hazardous spill and material cleanup company. We specialize in diesel fuel and petroleum based oil containment and cleanup.

License# 98000-105807-24-122024

  • HAZWOPER 40 certified technicians on staff

Spill equipment

Three spill trailers

Equipped with diesel pump off stations, pump sprayers, absorbents, booms and pools.

Roadway degreaser

A biohazard friendly degreaser that removes hydrocarbons from petroleum and oil spills through a chemical reaction, leaving only H2O and CO2, which is safe for storm water runoff.

Sweepers

Two skid steers with power sweeper attachments, plus a self contained 10 yard street sweeper.

Debris hauling

A tri-axle dump trailer for hauling debris from scenes.

Electric vehicle fires

Two EV fire blankets to help fire departments and protect our drivers and staff if a vehicle reignites during transport or storage.

EV storage area

An enclosed space with a three sided brick wall that protects other vehicles in the yard if an EV reignites.
