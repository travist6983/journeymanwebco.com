Privacy

Your information, in plain English.

Last updated September 22, 2026

What the form collects

When you ask for a free website review, the form sends me:

  • your name
  • your business name
  • your phone number
  • your email address
  • your current website, if you have one
  • any notes you add
  • the package you're interested in, if you picked one

Why I collect it

So I can reply to you about your website. That's the only reason.

Where it goes

  • Your request is emailed to me at travis@journeymanwebco.com through an email sending service.
  • Before the form sends, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a person, not a bot, is filling it in. To do that, Cloudflare looks at information about your browser and connection.
  • The site uses privacy-friendly analytics to count visits. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't follow you around other websites.

Links in my emails

If you got an email from me, the link in it has a short code so I know which business visited. While you're on this site it records the pages and buttons you use. No cookies, and nothing follows you to other sites. Email me and I'll delete it.

What I don't do

I never sell your information.

Deleting your information

Want me to delete what you sent? Email travis@journeymanwebco.com and I'll take care of it.
